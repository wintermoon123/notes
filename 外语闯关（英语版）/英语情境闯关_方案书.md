# 英语情境闯关 · 方案书

> 承接《语言学习小游戏 v2》，把核心玩法从「三段式关卡」替换为**单线情境闯关**：
> 10 个真实英语情境 → 每关 8 个决策点 → 答对推进进度条 → 进度达 90% 即任务成功。
> 形态：浏览器直接打开的 HTML 单页（`index.html` + `data/questions.js`）。
> 积分预算：与 v2 共用 1000 分额度。

---

## 1. 一句话定位

**你不是在背单词，你是在用英语把一件事办成。**

每个情境给你一个明确目标（顺利用餐完毕 / 顺利完成一次旅行 / 搞定一次面试），一路 8 次抉择，每一次都靠英语表达推进。进度条就是任务完成度——不是分数。

---

## 2. 进度机制（核心规则）

### 2.1 规则表

| 项 | 普通情境（8 题） | 复杂情境（10 题） |
|---|---|---|
| 进入情境初始进度 | **20%** | **20%** |
| 每个决策点 | 答对 **+10%**，答错 **+0%** | 答对 **+8%**，答错 **+0%** |
| 单情境决策点数 | 8 个 | 10 个 |
| 理论最高进度 | 100% | 100% |
| **达标线** | **90%** | **90%** |
| 达标所需 | 8 题至少对 7 题 | 10 题至少对 9 题 |
| **容错** | **1 题** | **1 题** |

> **为什么按题数调整单题分值？** 若统一用「起始 20% + 每题 10%」，10 题的情境会溢出到 120%，封顶后又会让「对 8 题」和「对 10 题」都显示 100%，失去区分度。改为每题 8% 后，两类情境**容错都是 1 题**，机制一致，进度也不会溢出。

**哪些是复杂情境（10 题）**：情境 7 航班延误改签 · 情境 8 求职面试 · 情境 9 会议汇报 —— 这三个场景的决策链更长（改签要谈费用+行李、面试要谈薪资+反问、汇报要应对追问+收尾），8 题撑不住。

### 2.2 四种结局

| 进度 | 条件（普通 8 题 / 复杂 10 题） | 结局表现 | 后续 |
|---|---|---|---|
| **100%** | 全对 | 目标圆满完成，NPC 给最高评价 | 解锁下一关 + 生词本新增 0 条 |
| **90%** | 错 1 题 | 目标完成，但过程中有波折 | 解锁下一关 + 错题进生词本 |
| **80%** | 错 2 题 | 目标未完成 | **可重打本情境**（题目相同，不耗 AI 积分） |
| **≤70%** | 错 3 题及以上 | 任务失败 | 可重打 + 全部错题进生词本 + 展开讲解 |

> **为什么容错只有 1 题？** 因为答错不扣分、只能靠后面追——落后是「不可逆」的。这是你的规则带来的必然结果，也是它有张力的原因。**这反过来要求题目必须足够清晰**：干扰项要明确错在语法、搭配、答非所问或语域，不能出现「两个都对」的选项。
>
> 若想要更宽松，备选变体：起始进度改为 10%、每题仍 +10% → 需对 8 题，容错 2 题。默认按上表执行。

### 2.3 进度条上的三道刻度

| 刻度 | 含义 | 界面反馈 |
|---|---|---|
| 30% | 顺利 | 常规配色 |
| 60% | 磕碰 | 进度条转琥珀色，提示「有点麻烦了」 |
| 90% | **达成** | 进度条转绿，达标线亮起脉冲 |

### 2.4 重打机制

- 未达标（<90%）→ 结算页给「再试一次」按钮，**题目完全相同、不消耗任何 AI 积分**。
- 重打不叠加分数，只看最高进度；生词本取并集。
- 理由：同一情境重复失败对学习者无益，而重复消耗 AI 会烧积分。

## 4. 题库：80 个决策点

> **出题纪律 v3（2026-11-16 二次修订 · 现行版本）**
>
> **v2 的问题**：干扰项虽然语法正确，但仍被写成「正确答案的换句话说」。
> 例：咖啡店 DP4 的 A「I would prefer whole」/ B「Whole would be great」/ D「Whole milk, please」——三个选项是同一句话。
> 玩家只能靠「哪句语法最干净」排除，而不是靠「此刻该说什么」判断——**而这正是语言学习最该练的能力**。
> 实测：86 题中 46 题（53%）存在此类问题。
>
> **v3 核心范式：决策分岔型**
>
> 四个选项 = **四种不同的真实交际选择**，而不是同一意图的四种措辞。
>
> | 层次 | 缺陷 | 例子（咖啡店 DP1） |
> |---|---|---|
> | ✅ | 正确项：完成当前任务 | `Could I get a large iced latte, please?` |
> | **L1** | 转移话题——问了另一件事 | `Actually, do you sell any pastries?` |
> | **L2** | 改需求——做了另一件事 | `Actually, could I get that hot instead?` |
> | **L3** | 无关回应——说的是自己的事 | `Sorry, I need to check my schedule first.` |
>
> **四条硬规则**
> 1. **意图互斥** —— 四个选项表达四种不同的交际动作
> 2. **场景合法** —— 三个干扰项都是真实对话里说得出口的话，语法正确
> 3. **唯一最优** —— 只凭「这句在此刻合不合适」就能选出答案，不靠语感排除
> 4. **微差异禁用** —— 不使用「只差一个词」的干扰项（`with please` vs `please`），差异必须体现在语义而非拼写
>
> **配套约束**：正确项与 L1 项都带礼貌标记，避免形成「选带 please 的」这种无效策略。
> 约 20% 的题目额外加入 1 个「中文母语者负迁移」干扰项。
>
> **解析改为内容指代格式**：不用字母指代选项，改用该选项的短句（反引号包裹），
> 因此答案位置重排时解析永远不会错位。
>
> **机器验收**：`tools/check_distinctness.py` 以实义词 Jaccard 为主判据（阈值 0.7），
> 检出不通过就重排。当前 **86 题 / 0 题不通过**。
>
> 详细规范见《选项重写规范.md》，验收清单见《干扰项验收清单.md》，语料出处见《教学点索引.md》。
>
> **答案位置**：每情境 `A C B D` 循环 → 全局 A/C 各 23、B/D 各 20。

### 情境 1 · 咖啡店点单（CEFR A2）
**目标**：点一杯符合自己偏好的饮品并顺利结账。
**考点覆盖**：点单请求 · 规格选择 · 需求表达 · 替代方案 · 婉拒 · 付款 · 收据 · 道别

#### DP1 · 店员问你要什么 | 考点：点单请求句式
> NPC：「Hi there! What can I get started for you?」

- **A. Could I get a large iced latte, please?** ✅
- B. Actually, do you sell any pastries?
- C. Actually, could I get that hot instead?
- D. Sorry, I need to check my schedule first.

**解析**：正确项用 `Could I get…please` 完成点单，是咖啡店最地道的说法。`sell any pastries`转移到了另一件事上——店员要你说的是「要什么」，不是反问店里有什么甜点。`get that hot instead`改了需求（要热的），没有完成点单。`need to check my schedule first`说的是自己的安排，完全没有点单。

#### DP2 · 冷热选择 | 考点：省略与礼貌副词
> NPC：「Would you like that hot or iced?」

- A. Is the iced one stronger?
- B. Actually, make it a double.
- **C. Iced, please.** ✅
- D. Cold one, thanks.

**解析**：正确项是服务业的标准省略说法——只说名词加 `please` 就够了。`the iced one stronger`改了规格（要双份），不是回答冷热。`make it a double`的 `cold` 泛指「冷的」，而热饮是 `hot`、冰饮才是 `iced`，店员可能听不懂。`Cold one, thanks`问了另一个问题，没有回答冷热。

#### DP3 · 换一种奶 | 考点：否定 + 替代提议
> NPC：「Would you like oat milk in that?」

- A. Yes, put the oat milk in.
- **B. No, regular milk is fine, thanks.** ✅
- C. Actually, could I get that as a tea instead?
- D. Oat is good, thank you.

**解析**：正确项先否定、再给替代、最后致谢，三步齐全，是最完整的回应。`put the oat milk in`方向相反——你其实不想要燕麦奶。`get that as a tea instead`改成了完全不同的饮品。`Oat is good, thank you`语义含糊，听不出是同意还是拒绝。

#### DP4 · 缺货时改选 | 考点：询问替代品
> NPC：「I'm sorry, we're out of oat milk today. Would you like whole or skim?」

- A. Do you have any other kinds of milk?
- B. Actually, I'll have it black, no milk.
- C. Whatever you have is fine.
- **D. Whole milk, please.** ✅

**解析**：正确项直接给出选择并加 `please`，店员不用再追问。`have any other kinds of milk`问了另一个问题，没有做出选择。`I'll have it black, no milk`把需求整个改掉了（改成黑咖啡）。`Whatever you have is fine`回避选择——在缺货已经发生、需要明确选项的时候说这个，等于把选择推回给店员。

#### DP5 · 要收据 | 考点：礼貌索取
> NPC：「That'll be $5.40. Would you like a receipt?」

- **A. Yes, could I have a receipt, please?** ✅
- B. No, thank you — I've got my card statement.
- C. Could I get one of those stamps instead?
- D. Could you reply me a receipt?

**解析**：正确项用 `could I have` 请求，是索取类动作的标准句式。`thank you — I've got my card statement`拒绝了——但你需要收据做报销或保修。`get one of those stamps instead`问了完全不相干的东西。`reply me a receipt`是搭配错误：`reply` 不能接人作宾语（`reply me` 是中文母语者高频错误），英语要说 `reply to me` 或换 `give me`。

#### DP6 · 付款方式 | 考点：介词短语与请托
> NPC：「You can pay by card or cash. How would you like to pay?」

- A. Is there a card fee?
- B. Could you just charge it to my card instead?
- **C. By card, please.** ✅
- D. Use my card.

**解析**：正确项用 `by card` 这个介词短语表示支付方式，最简洁得体。`a card fee`问了费用而不是回答问题。`just charge it to my card instead`把说法改成「记我卡上」，改变了含义。`Use my card`是祈使命令——在收银台对店员说 `Use my card` 相当不礼貌。

#### DP7 · 要袋子 | 考点：接受与致谢
> NPC：「Would you like a bag for that?」

- A. No, I'll carry it carefully.
- **B. Yes, a bag would be great, thanks.** ✅
- C. What kind of bags do you have?
- D. Yes bag.

**解析**：正确项完整自然，致谢到位。`I'll carry it carefully`没必要拒绝——店员在提供便利。`kind of bags do you have`问了店员已经回答过的问题。`bag`缺冠词（英语单数可数名词一般不能裸用），句子读起来断裂。

#### DP8 · 道别 | 考点：回应祝福
> NPC：「Enjoy your latte! Have a good day.」

- A. Yes, I enjoy my latte very much.
- B. Could I have another one for the road?
- C. Sorry, I was in a hurry this morning.
- **D. Thanks, you too. Have a great day!** ✅

**解析**：正确项用 `you too` 回应对方的祝愿，是英语道别的固定动作。`enjoy my latte very much`只是附和，没接住祝愿。`have another one for the road`又要了一杯，把道别变成新任务。`was in a hurry this morning`说了句无关的话，也没回应祝愿。

### 情境 2 · 餐厅晚餐（CEFR A2–B1）
**目标**：完成点餐、用餐与结账，并解决账单疑问。
**考点覆盖**：点前菜 · 询问推荐 · 点主菜 · 询问食材与过敏 · 请求服务 · 分单 · 指出错误 · 离店

#### DP1 · 点前菜 | 考点：Could we 请求句式
> NPC：「Good evening. Are you ready to order?」

- **A. Could we start with the soup to share, please?** ✅
- B. Are there any specials today?
- C. I'll have whatever is quickest.
- D. We'd like the soup to start, please.

**解析**：正确项用 `Could we…please` 提请求，`to share` 顺带说明意图，是餐厅点前菜的完整说法。`any specials today`问了另一个问题，没有点单。`I'll have whatever is quickest`把选择权交给餐厅，不算真正点单。`We'd like the soup to start, please`语法没错，但没有 `Could`，在服务场景里显得生硬。

#### DP2 · 请对方推荐 | 考点：征询建议
> NPC：「Our chef's specialty tonight is the grilled sea bass.」

- A. Is the sea bass quite spicy?
- B. I'll just order the sea bass then.
- **C. That sounds good — what would you recommend?** ✅
- D. Our options for the fish are quite limited.

**解析**：正确项先接住对方的话（`That sounds good`），再用 `what would you recommend` 征询建议，是英语餐厅里的自然推进方式。`the sea bass quite spicy`问了菜的口味，不是征求推荐。`I'll just order the sea bass then`直接定了，没利用推荐机会。`Our options for the fish are quite limited`是公文腔，不像在点菜时说的话。

#### DP3 · 点主菜 | 考点：点菜固定表达
> NPC：「And for your main course?」

- A. What's the difference between the salmon and the steak?
- **B. I'll have the grilled salmon, please.** ✅
- C. Could I have the salmon without the sauce?
- D. Give me grilled salmon.

**解析**：正确项用 `I'll have the…`——英式/澳式餐厅点主菜的标准说法，礼貌自然。`What's the difference between the salmon and the`问了对比而不是点单。`have the salmon without the sauce`提出了额外要求，但主菜还没点定。`Give me grilled salmon`是祈使命令，很冲。

#### DP4 · 询问过敏与食材 | 考点：提出健康相关询问
> NPC：「Before I bring that over — do you have any allergies?」

- A. Is the sauce made with peanuts?
- B. Actually, I'll have the salad instead.
- C. I'm allergic to nuts and shellfish.
- **D. Yes — I'm allergic to peanuts. Could you make sure the sauce has none?** ✅

**解析**：正确项明确说出过敏原、再提具体请求——这是真实场景里必须说清的两件事。`the sauce made with peanuts`问了配料，虽然有用但没表明自己的过敏情况。`I'll have the salad instead`回避了问题（改成沙拉）。`I'm allergic to nuts and shellfish`只列了过敏原，没有请求厨房确认，风险更大。

#### DP5 · 餐中请求 | 考点：提出请求
> NPC：「How is everything tasting so far?」

- **A. It's lovely, thank you. Could we have some more bread, please?** ✅
- B. Is everything all right with the kitchen?
- C. Actually, this isn't quite what I ordered.
- D. I'm afraid the soup was rather disappointing.

**解析**：正确项先回应对方的问题、再提请求，节奏完整。`everything all right with the kitchen`问了另一个问题。`this isn't quite what I ordered`提出了问题但没说要什么。`I'm afraid the soup was rather disappointing`是抱怨，没推进任务——而且如果真不满意，应该更直接地提出要换菜。

#### DP6 · 结账分单 | 考点：礼貌提出分账
> NPC：「Would you like to settle the bill now?」

- A. Can we pay separately?
- B. Actually, is it possible to move this to a table for four?
- **C. Could we have the bill, and could you split it for us, please?** ✅
- D. What time is your kitchen closing?

**解析**：正确项用两个 `could` 提请求，并说清「分单」的具体诉求，是真实场景需要的完整表达。`pay separately`太简略，没说清怎么分。`possible to move this to a table for`提出了完全不同的要求。`time is your kitchen closing`问了不相关的问题。

#### DP7 · 账单有误 | 考点：礼貌指出问题
> NPC：「Here you are — that's $86 including the bottle of wine.」

- A. Could you make it eighty-six, please?
- **B. I'm sorry, I think there's a mistake — we didn't order the wine.** ✅
- C. Is there a bottle of water included in that too?
- D. Who ordered the wine?

**解析**：正确项先致歉缓冲、再陈述事实，是服务场景指出问题的标准结构。`make it eighty-six, please`试图直接改金额。`a bottle of water included in that too`问了另一笔账。`Who ordered the wine`把责任推给不在场的同事。

#### DP8 · 离店 | 考点：致谢与道别
> NPC：「Thank you, and we hope you enjoyed your evening.」

- A. Yes, the food was lovely. What is your name again?
- B. Could we book a table for next Thursday?
- C. We're sorry, we couldn't quite finish the wine.
- **D. Thank you, we enjoyed it very much. Have a good evening!** ✅

**解析**：正确项完整呼应对方的祝愿。`the food was lovely. What is your name`问了服务员姓名，很突兀。`book a table for next Thursday`顺势订下次，是真实但跑偏的回应（此刻任务是结账离店）。`We're sorry, we couldn't quite finish the wine`说了句无关且略带暗示的话。

### 情境 3 · 酒店入住（CEFR A2–B1）
**目标**：顺利办入住，并解决一个住宿问题。
**考点覆盖**：预订确认 · 核对日期 · 提出要求 · 报告问题 · 询问升级 · 询问设施 · 延迟退房 · 道别

#### DP1 · 报预订 | 考点：under + 姓氏
> NPC：「Good evening. Do you have a reservation with us?」

- **A. Yes, I have a reservation under the name Chen.** ✅
- B. What time is your check-in?
- C. I need a room for two nights, I think.
- D. Please give me a room with a booking.

**解析**：正确项用 `under the name + 姓`——酒店预订的标准说法。`time is your check-in`问了另一个问题。`need a room for two nights, I think`没有提供姓名，等于没说清自己是谁。`give me a room with a booking`是搭配错误，`give me a room with a booking` 说不通。

#### DP2 · 核对日期 | 考点：确认信息
> NPC：「That's for three nights. Just to confirm, checking out on Friday?」

- A. Sorry, could you repeat that?
- B. Actually, I'd like to change it to Saturday.
- **C. That's right — check-out on Friday the 14th.** ✅
- D. Yes yes, Friday, correct.

- C. Actually, could you put me on a later flight?
- D. OK, I will take that one.

**解析**：正确项用 `Are there any other options…` 打开更多可能——处理延误的核心句式，能问出比客服主动推荐更多的选项。`a bus to the city from the airport`问了另一个问题。`put me on a later flight`提了不同诉求。`OK, I will take that one`直接接受，没争取。

#### DP4 · 争取补偿 | 考点：提出补偿要求
> NPC：「As a delay of this length, we can offer a meal voucher.」

- A. Could you tell me where to get the meal voucher?
- B. Actually, could you upgrade me to business class?
- C. A meal voucher is fine, thanks.
- **D. Could I also get hotel accommodation and transport for the night?** ✅

**解析**：正确项用 `Could I also get…` 在已有方案上追加请求，是最地道的谈判句式——注意 `also` 明确表示「在此基础上再加」。`tell me where to get the meal voucher`问了兑换地点。`upgrade me to business class`提了升级诉求。`A meal voucher is fine, thanks`放弃争取。

#### DP5 · 确认细节 | 考点：核对信息
> NPC：「Your new flight departs at 09:15 from gate 22.」

- **A. Let me confirm — 09:15 tomorrow, gate 22, and my baggage is checked through?** ✅
- B. Is there a lounge I could use while I wait?
- C. Actually, could you move me to a later flight instead?
- D. Yes, 09:15 from gate 22.

**解析**：正确项复述全部关键信息并追问行李，是稳妥的专业做法——改签最容易出错的就是时间和行李。`a lounge I could use while I wait`问了另一个问题。`move me to a later flight instead`提了不同诉求。`09:15 from gate 22`只确认了时间，漏掉行李这个关键点。

#### DP6 · 行李安排 | 考点：确认行李处理
> NPC：「Your checked bag will be transferred automatically.」

- A. How long does baggage claim usually take?
- B. Actually, could you send my bag to my hotel instead?
- **C. Great — and I'll collect it at baggage claim on arrival, not at the transfer desk?** ✅
- D. Good, it's automatic.

**解析**：正确项完整复述并确认细节，是旅客应该做的核对——`at baggage claim` 是关键信息。`How long does baggage claim usually take`问了另一个问题。`send my bag to my hotel instead`提了不同诉求。`it's automatic`只确认没问清关键细节。

#### DP7 · 登机时间 | 考点：询问时间
> NPC：「Boarding opens two hours before departure.」

- A. Is there a duty-free shop near the gate?
- **B. So boarding opens at 07:15 — should I go straight to security?** ✅
- C. Actually, could you hold my flight for an hour?
- D. Boarding opens at 07:15.

**解析**：正确项复述时间并追问下一步，是最有用的问法。`a duty-free shop near the gate`问了另一个问题。`hold my flight for an hour`提了不同诉求。`Boarding opens at 07:15`只复述没追问，浪费了一个可以确认流程的机会。

#### DP8 · 结束 | 考点：致谢与收尾
> NPC：「We're sorry again for the disruption. Here's your new boarding pass.」

- A. Could you also print me a copy of the receipt?
- B. Actually, could you move me to the 11:00 flight?
- C. Thanks very much.
- **D. Thanks for sorting that out so quickly — it's been a long day, but I appreciate the help.** ✅

**解析**：正确项完整且符合真实情境的情绪节奏——一天被延误折腾，最后一句 `I appreciate the help` 才说得诚恳。`also print me a copy of the receipt`提出了新请求。`move me to the 11:00 flight`提了不同诉求。`Thanks very much`太简略，没接住对方的道歉。

#### DP9 · 追问改签费用 | 考点：询问附加条件
> NPC：「I can move you to tomorrow's 09:15 flight. There is a change fee of 50 dollars.」

- **A. Does the change fee apply if the delay is on your side?** ✅
- B. Could you tell me where to get the meal voucher?
- C. Actually, could you upgrade me to business class?
- D. A meal voucher is fine, thanks.

**解析**：正确项用 `Does…apply if…` 确认条款适用范围——因航空公司原因延误，往往可以主张免改签费，这是真实场景里的关键一问。`the change fee apply if the delay is on` 语法正确，但问的是「有没有别的航班」，没有触及费用这个真正的问题。A 转移话题，同样没问费用。D 语气生硬，且没了解规则就做决定。C 直接砍价在柜台通常无效，`make it lower` 也不合搭配（应为 `lower it`）。

#### DP10 · 行李直挂确认 | 考点：确认托运安排
> NPC：「Would you like to collect your checked bag at Frankfurt, or have it checked through?」

- A. How long does baggage claim usually take?
- B. Actually, could you send my bag to my hotel instead?
- **C. Please check it through to Osaka — I'll collect it there.** ✅
- D. Yes, I'll take my bag at Frankfurt.

**解析**：正确项用 `check it through to + 目的地` + `collect it there` 两句说清意图，是转机行李的标准表达。A 也说得通，但省略了「在哪里取」，客服还得追问一次。B 只说了不要在哪取，没说自己要怎么取，客服无从操作。D 语法正确，但否掉直挂后也没说清自己到底要怎样。

### 情境 8 · 求职面试（CEFR B1–B2）
**目标**：完成面试并进入下一轮。
**考点覆盖**：自我介绍 · 离职原因 · 优势 · 缺点 · 量化成就 · 薪资预期 · 反问 · 收尾

#### DP1 · 自我介绍 | 考点：结构化自我介绍
> NPC：「Thanks for coming in. So, tell me a bit about yourself.」

- **A. I'm a data analyst with three years in retail; I currently own our weekly forecasting model.** ✅
- B. Could you tell me what the main responsibilities are?
- C. Actually, could we start with the team introduction?
- D. I am very good person and I always work hard.

**解析**：正确项用「身份 + 经验年限 + 具体职责」三段式，是高效自我介绍的标准结构——面试官最想知道的是「你能做什么」。`tell me what the main responsibilities are`反问职责。`start with the team introduction`想跳过自我介绍。`am very good person and I always work`是中文母语者的典型负迁移（`very good person` 缺冠词），且空洞无证据。

#### DP2 · 离职原因 | 考点：专业地回答离职原因
> NPC：「What made you decide to leave your current role?」

- A. Could I ask what the team's biggest challenge is?
- B. Actually, I'd like to talk about my notice period.
- **C. I've reached the limit of what I can learn in that role, and I want work where I own a full product.** ✅
- D. My boss is very difficult person.

**解析**：正确项把动机落在成长与「完整负责一件事」上，是成熟的职业表达——既不贬低现雇主，又说明你为什么要来。`ask what the team's biggest challenge is`反问。`I'd like to talk about my notice period`急着谈离职时间，显得急。`My boss is very difficult person`抱怨雇主（还缺冠词），是最不该说的答案。

#### DP3 · 优势 | 考点：优势 + 证据
> NPC：「What would you say is your main strength?」

- A. Could you tell me what the biggest challenge here is?
- **B. I'm good at turning messy data into something a non-technical stakeholder can act on.** ✅
- C. Actually, could we move on to the next question?
- D. My strength is that I am very hard working.

**解析**：正确项具体说明了「做什么」与「对谁有价值」——`turn…into…` 和 `stakeholder` 都是职场高频词。`tell me what the biggest challenge here is`反问。`move on to the next question`想跳过问题。`My strength is that I am very hard`是没有证据的形容词堆砌。

#### DP4 · 缺点 | 考点：真实 + 改进
> NPC：「And what about a weakness?」

- A. Could I ask how you would rate my performance?
- B. Actually, I'd rather not answer that one.
- C. My weakness is I am a perfectionist sometimes.
- **D. I used to take on too much at once; now I prioritise and say no early.** ✅

**解析**：正确项承认真实问题、给出改进策略、并用 `now` 划出前后界限，是标准的高质量回答。`ask how you would rate my performance`反问评分。`I'd rather not answer that one`直接拒绝回答。`My weakness is I am a perfectionist sometimes`是「假缺点」模板——面试官听过一万次，会判为不真诚。

#### DP5 · 量化成就 | 考点：用数字说话
> NPC：「Tell me about a project you're proud of.」

- **A. I rebuilt our churn model; within two quarters, churn dropped from 9% to 6%.** ✅
- B. Could you tell me which projects would be most relevant here?
- C. Actually, could I describe a smaller project instead?
- D. I did a project about the customers and it was very good.

**解析**：正确项有具体动作（`rebuilt`）、明确时间（`within two quarters`）、可验证的结果（`from 9% to 6%`）。`tell me which projects would be most relevant`反问。`describe a smaller project instead`回避展示成果。`did a project about the customers and it`缺可验证的结果——面试官记住的是数字。

#### DP6 · 薪资预期 | 考点：讨论薪资
> NPC：「What are your salary expectations?」

- A. Could you tell me how the bonus structure works?
- B. Actually, I'd like to hear more about the team first.
- **C. I've seen the band for this role is around X — I'm flexible depending on the overall package.** ✅
- D. I want to get more than my current salary.

**解析**：正确项表明了解市场、带出区间、说明弹性（`the overall package` 是关键——谈薪谈的不只是数字），是稳妥的表达。`tell me how the bonus structure works`反问奖金。`I'd like to hear more about the team`回避了钱的问题。`want to get more than my current salary`只说方向没有信息量。

#### DP7 · 反问环节 | 考点：提出高质量问题
> NPC：「That's everything from me — do you have any questions for us?」

- A. Could you tell me what the office hours are?
- **B. What does success look like in this role after the first six months?** ✅
- C. Actually, could we go back to my experience?
- D. No, I think that covers everything from me.

**解析**：正确项问「成功标准」，展示对岗位的思考深度——`What does success look like…` 是面试反问的最高频优质句式。`tell me what the office hours are`问办公时间，偏个人利益。`go back to my experience`想回到自己的话题。`think that covers everything from me`放弃了提问机会，等于浪费了这一环。

#### DP8 · 收尾 | 考点：结束致谢
> NPC：「We'll be in touch by next Friday. Thanks for your time today.」

- A. Could you tell me when exactly I'll hear from you?
- B. Actually, could we set up a second interview now?
- C. Thank you very much, goodbye.
- **D. Thank you for your time — I really enjoyed learning about the role. Have a good day.** ✅

**解析**：正确项完整并有温度——`I enjoyed learning about the role` 显示你是来了解岗位而不是只想要 offer。`tell me when exactly I'll hear from you`追问时间，显得急。`set up a second interview now`想当场再约。`Thank you very much, goodbye`太简略，没接住 `Thanks for your time` 的收尾。

#### DP9 · 争取额外支持 | 考点：表达诉求并留退路
> NPC：「Let me know if there's anything else you need before we close the call.」

- **A. Could you send me the reimbursement policy in writing, so I have it for my records?** ✅
- B. Could you tell me what the office hours are?
- C. Actually, I'd like to talk about my salary now.
- D. No, nothing else, thank you very much.

**解析**：正确项用 `Could you…` 请求、并用 `so I have it for my records` 说明用途——两句话把「要什么」和「为什么要」都讲清，是职场沟通的标准做法。`nothing else, thank you very much` 过于简略。C 答非所问且语气生硬。D 是祈使命令（`You should…`），对客服说很不合适。

#### DP10 · 约定后续跟进 | 考点：确认下一步
> NPC：「I'll email you the updated quote by end of day.」

- A. Could you tell me when exactly I'll hear from you?
- B. Actually, could we set up a second interview now?
- **C. Perfect — could you send it to my work address, and cc me on the invoice?** ✅
- D. Good. I will wait for your email then, thank you.

**解析**：正确项先回应、再提出两个具体请求（发到哪个地址、抄送发票），是「推进任务」的标准表达。A/B 只回应不推进，没有确认具体交付方式。D 语气生硬（`I hope you send it soon` 暗含责备），而且没有确认任何事情。

### 情境 9 · 会议汇报（CEFR B1–B2）
**目标**：完成汇报并回应提问。
**考点覆盖**：开场 · 过渡提示 · 被打断 · 尖锐提问 · 不确定时模糊限定 · 承认不知道 · 提出行动项 · 总结

#### DP1 · 开场 | 考点：结构化开场
> NPC：「Right, let's move on to the quarterly results. Over to you.」

- **A. Thanks, Alex. I'll cover three things: last quarter's numbers, what drove them, and what we're doing next.** ✅
- B. Could you tell me which metric matters most to you?
- C. Actually, could we skip straight to the actions?
- D. Yes, I will talk about the numbers now.

**解析**：正确项一句预告结构，让听众知道怎么听——`I'll cover three things` 是汇报开场的黄金句式。`tell me which metric matters most to you`反问。`skip straight to the actions`想跳过铺垫。`will talk about the numbers now`没有结构（听众不知道要听多久）。

#### DP2 · 过渡 | 考点：过渡信号词
> NPC：「（翻页）So — where does that leave us?」

- A. Could you remind me what the second driver was?
- B. Actually, could we go back to the revenue slide?
- **C. Turning to why the numbers moved, there are two main drivers.** ✅
- D. Now I want to talk about the next part.

**解析**：正确项用 `Turning to…`——专业的过渡信号词，并且预告「两个原因」让听众能跟上。`remind me what the second driver was`反问刚讲过的内容。`go back to the revenue slide`想翻回去。`Now I want to talk about the next`是随口一说，没有过渡功能。

#### DP3 · 被打断 | 考点：礼貌应对打断
> NPC：「Sorry to jump in — can you explain that revenue drop on slide four?」

- A. Could you hold that until I've finished the section?
- **B. Sure, happy to — let me take that one now.** ✅
- C. Actually, could we go back to slide three?
- D. Yes, okay. Slide four, revenue, drop.

**解析**：正确项立刻转向提问者，态度合作——`happy to` + `let me take that one now` 是英语应对打断的标准组合。`hold that until I've finished the section`是请求，语气偏硬。`go back to slide three`转移了话题。`okay. Slide four, revenue, drop`只是复述词，没真正回应。

#### DP4 · 尖锐提问 | 考点：承认局限
> NPC：「Honestly, this forecast looks optimistic. How do you justify it?」

- A. Could you tell me what a realistic forecast looks like?
- B. Actually, could we use last year's actuals instead?
- C. It is correct, trust me, the data is right.
- **D. That's fair — it does assume no further churn. Here's how sensitive it is to that assumption.** ✅

**解析**：正确项先认同对方质疑合理（`That's fair`），再承认假设（`it does assume…`），最后给出应对——三层递进，是处理尖锐提问的高分答案。`tell me what a realistic forecast looks like`反问。`use last year's actuals instead`提议换数据，是换话题不是回答。`correct, trust me, the data is right`硬顶。

#### DP5 · 不确定时 | 考点：模糊限定语
> NPC：「Do you think customers will accept the new pricing?」

- **A. I think they probably will, though it depends on how we position it.** ✅
- B. Could you tell me what the sales team thinks?
- C. Actually, could we run a survey first?
- D. Yes they will accept, I am sure about it.

**解析**：正确项用 `I think / probably / it depends on`——既给出判断又留有余地，是英语母语者回答不确定问题的典型方式（`hedging` 是职场英语核心技能）。`tell me what the sales team thinks`反问。`run a survey first`提议新工作，是拖延。`they will accept, I am sure about it`过于绝对，在管理层面前容易翻车。

#### DP6 · 承认不知道 | 考点：诚实的边界
> NPC：「Do you know the churn rate for the SME segment specifically?」

- A. Could you tell me who prepared this deck?
- B. Actually, could we skip this slide?
- **C. I don't have that figure in front of me — I'll follow up after the meeting.** ✅
- D. I think it is around 4 percent, yes.

**解析**：正确项诚实说明并承诺跟进时间，是专业底线——`I don't have that figure in front of me` 是职场英语里最有分量的诚实句式。`tell me who prepared this deck`反问制作人。`skip this slide`想跳过。`think it is around 4 percent, yes`猜测（数据问题上猜错代价很大）。

#### DP7 · 提出行动项 | 考点：明确行动与责任人
> NPC：「So — what are we doing next?」

- A. Could you tell me who's already assigned to this?
- **B. Two things: Sarah takes the pricing page by Thursday, and I'll circulate the revised model on Monday.** ✅
- C. Actually, could we defer this to the next meeting?
- D. We will see later, maybe something.

**解析**：正确项具体到人（Sarah / I）、到物（pricing page / revised model）、到时间（Thursday / Monday），是行动项的标准写法。`tell me who's already assigned to this`反问。`defer this to the next meeting`提议推迟，等于没推进。`will see later, maybe something`太模糊，没有负责人也没有时间。

#### DP8 · 总结 | 考点：收束要点
> NPC：「Right. Anything else before we close?」

- A. Could you tell me what the deadline for the pricing page is?
- B. Actually, could we schedule a follow-up meeting?
- C. No, nothing else, thanks very much.
- **D. Just to recap: revenue down 3%, churn is the driver, and Sarah's pricing page lands Thursday.** ✅

**解析**：正确项用 `Just to recap` 收束并重复三项要点，是高效会议的结尾——听众散会后记住的就是这三句。`tell me what the deadline for the pricing`问了刚说过的事。`schedule a follow-up meeting`提议另开会，等于没推进。`nothing else, thanks very much`只是结束动作，没做收束。

#### DP9 · 接受追问 | 考点：承认限制并给替代
> NPC：「You mentioned the model is preliminary. Do you have a validated version yet?」

- **A. Not yet — the model is still preliminary, but I'll have a validated version by Friday.** ✅
- B. Could you tell me who prepared this deck?
- C. Actually, could we skip this slide?
- D. Yes, I have the final version, I can send it today.

**解析**：正确项用 `Not yet` 直接回应是非问句（而不是绕开），再给出明确的时间承诺——`I'll have…by Friday` 是职场英语的标准承诺句式。B 语序错（`do not have…yet` 应在助动词后立刻否定，但这里缺 `I` 的位置尚可，问题在 `I will have it by Friday` 缺少将来时标记的完整结构）。`have the final version, I can send it today` 撒谎。D 是话轮功能错——对方问有没有、答的却是"我不做这个"，答非所问。

#### DP10 · 收束会议 | 考点：总结与确认
> NPC：「That's all from me. Anything else before we wrap up?」

- A. Could you tell me what the deadline for the pricing page is?
- B. Actually, could we schedule a follow-up meeting?
- **C. One thing — let's confirm who's taking the pricing page by Thursday.** ✅
- D. No, I think that is everything, thank you.

**解析**：正确项用 `One thing` 引出并落到具体行动项（谁做、周几前），是收尾会议的标准句式——既回应了对方，又推进了任务。A 答非所问（对方问有没有事要补充，答的却是要谈休假）。`think that is everything, thank you` 过于简略，且没确认关键行动项。C 语法正确，但答的是对演示的评价，没有推进任何事。

### 情境 10 · 职场求助与拒绝（CEFR B1–B2）
**目标**：既帮到同事，又守住自己的工作边界。
**考点覆盖**：回应请求 · 澄清范围 · 拒绝 · 提供替代 · 向上沟通 · 设边界 · 修复关系 · 跟进

#### DP1 · 同事的请求 | 考点：接受请求
> NPC：「Hey — are you free for a coffee? I've got a proposal due and I'm completely stuck.」

- **A. Sure, I've got a gap at 3 — what do you need to work through?** ✅
- B. Could you tell me what the proposal is about?
- C. Actually, could you send me the document first?
- D. No, I am very busy now.

**解析**：正确项给出具体时间（`a gap at 3`）并追问需求（`what do you need to work through?`）——承诺明确又留了余地。`tell me what the proposal is about`反问。`send me the document first`先要文件，是先要资源不是先帮忙。`am very busy now`生硬回绝。

#### DP2 · 澄清范围 | 考点：确认任务细节
> NPC：「Could you look over the whole proposal? It's about 30 pages.」

- A. Could you tell me who else is reviewing it?
- B. Actually, could you split it into two halves?
- **C. I can give it a proper read — would it help most if I focus on the financials?** ✅
- D. Yes, thirty pages I read all of it.

**解析**：正确项先承诺投入，再引导优先级——`would it help most if…` 这种建设性提问是协作的核心，比单纯答「好」有价值得多。`tell me who else is reviewing it`反问。`split it into two halves`提议拆分，是先谈工作量。`thirty pages I read all of it`语序散乱。

#### DP3 · 拒绝 | 考点：礼貌拒绝
> NPC：「Actually, could you also present it on Friday? I really can't do the talking.」

- A. Could you tell me what the presentation is for?
- **B. I'm afraid I can't present it — I'm already on another project that week.** ✅
- C. Actually, could we move Friday's meeting instead?
- D. No, I don't want to, no time for this.

**解析**：正确项用 `I'm afraid` 缓冲、给理由、不归因对方——是英语中拒绝的标准结构，三个要素缺一不可。`tell me what the presentation is for`反问。`move Friday's meeting instead`提议改期，是另一个方案但没回应「能不能做」。`don't want to, no time for this`生硬回绝。

#### DP4 · 提供替代 | 考点：提出替代方案
> NPC：「Any chance you could help with the numbers section?」

- A. Could you tell me who built the model?
- B. Actually, could we just use last quarter's numbers?
- C. No I cannot do the numbers.
- **D. I can't redo them, but I can sanity-check your assumptions if that helps.** ✅

**解析**：正确项说清不能做什么，同时给出自己能做的部分——`sanity-check` 是职场英语里既专业又精准的词，是合作型拒绝，比直接拒绝更有价值。`tell me who built the model`反问。`just use last quarter's numbers`提议用旧数据，是另一个方向。`cannot do the numbers`直接回绝，不留任何余地。

#### DP5 · 向上沟通 | 考点：向上级提出问题
> NPC：「My manager says we have to have this by tomorrow morning.」

- **A. If it's a hard deadline, could you let my manager know what's feasible? I'd rather set a realistic date than miss it.** ✅
- B. Could you tell me who set that deadline?
- C. Actually, could you take it off my desk for today?
- D. That's impossible, I have other work.

**解析**：正确项把冲突转成「谁来沟通 + 为什么」，并给出建设性落点——这是真正管用的表达。`tell me who set that deadline`反问。`take it off my desk for today`想甩掉工作。`That's impossible, I have other work`制造对抗。

#### DP6 · 设边界 | 考点：明确拒绝的范围
> NPC：「Are you sure you can't squeeze in an hour today?」

- A. Could you tell me what else is on your plate?
- B. Actually, could we get someone else to take it on?
- **C. Not today — I'm at capacity. Tomorrow afternoon I could make time.** ✅
- D. Yes, one hour, but after 6 pm.

**解析**：正确项保持立场的同时给出替代时间，是「有边界的友好」——`I'm at capacity` 是职场英语里体面的说法。`tell me what else is on your plate`反问。`get someone else to take it on`把活推给第三方。`one hour, but after 6 pm`先答应又加条件，会被继续追问。

#### DP7 · 修复关系 | 考点：致谢与推进
> NPC：「Thanks, I know you're slammed. I really appreciate it.」

- A. Could you tell me what the deadline is for my part?
- **B. Of course — let me know how it goes, and I'll check in with you tomorrow.** ✅
- C. Actually, could we do the same for you next time?
- D. No problem, no need to say thanks.

**解析**：正确项接受致谢并主动跟进，是关系维护的收尾——`check in with you tomorrow` 体现持续关注。`tell me what the deadline is for my`反问。`the same for you next time`提了交换条件，破坏了人情。`problem, no need to say thanks`把话说死，反而显得生硬。

#### DP8 · 跟进 | 考点：主动跟进
> NPC：「（第二天）Morning! Did you get a chance to look at the section?」

- A. Could you tell me who else is reviewing this section?
- B. Actually, could you look at it instead of me?
- C. Yes, I did it last night, here the changes.
- **D. Yes — I've left comments in the margin, mainly on the assumptions. Shall we run through it at 11?** ✅

**解析**：正确项说明做了什么（`left comments in the margin`）、留下可核查的痕迹、并提出具体下一步（`at 11`）。`tell me who else is reviewing this section`反问。`look at it instead of me`想甩回去。`did it last night, here the changes`简单交付——合理但没有留下任何可追踪的东西。

## 7. 文件结构与工程约定

```
语言学习闯关/
├── index.html          ← 界面 + 逻辑（HTML/CSS/JS 全内联）
└── data/
    ├── questions.js    ← 80 道题（window.QUESTIONS = [...]）
    └── scenes.js       ← 10 个情境（window.SCENES = [...]）
```

**为什么拆成两个文件**：题目是内容、逻辑是程序。分开后**改题不动代码、改代码不动题**，迭代时能省掉大量重复生成——这直接关系到积分成本。

**为什么仍是「HTML 形式」**：用经典 `<script src>` 标签（非 ES module），双击 `index.html` 用 `file://` 直接打开就能玩，**不需要起服务器**。

**关键约定**
- 状态集中管理：单一 `gameState` 对象，关卡切换不丢进度。
- 纯前端，零外部依赖、零 CDN 请求、零外部字体。
- 装饰全部用 CSS/SVG，不接图像生成（省积分 + 省体积）。
- 响应式：桌面双栏、移动单栏，触摸可点。
- 底部显示「本次会话 AI 调用次数」计数器。

## 9. 积分预算（更新版）

### 9.1 生成期

| 内容 | 产出规模 | 说明 |
|---|---|---|
| 80 道题 + 10 情境 | 约 14–18K token | **内容主体**，分批写、增量追加，避免整篇重写 |
| `index.html` 逻辑与界面 | 约 6–8K token | 一次写完，之后只做定点 Edit |
| 联调与修正 | 约 3–5K token | 用 Edit 改单点，不重发全文 |
| **合计** | 约 25–32K 输出 token | |

**省积分的三条做法**
1. **题库与代码分离**（§7）——改题只重写 `questions.js`，不动 `index.html`。
2. **分批追加**——先写情境 1–3 并跑通，再补 4–7，再补 8–10，每次只增量。
3. **反馈只做一次**——先在浏览器验证交互，再一次性补齐全部 80 题解析。

### 9.2 运行期

| 场景 | AI 调用 | 输出上限 |
|---|---|---|
| 首次玩某情境 | 1（A1 结算点评） | 600 token |
| 再玩同情境同一进度档 | 0（缓存） | 0 |
| 手动自由作答 / 生词卡 | 1（手动） | 600 token |

### 9.3 分配建议

| 区块 | 占比 | 说明 |
|---|---|---|
| 生成期 | ≈ 55% | 主体是 80 道题的内容生产 |
| 运行期 | ≈ 25% | 玩家实际游玩产生 |
| 缓冲 | ≈ 20% | 联调修正与打磨 |

> 官方未公开每千 token 的积分单价，实际扣减请在客户端「设置 → 积分」查看。本方案的控制手段是**调用次数、单次输出上限、是否缓存**这三件可精确计量的事。
