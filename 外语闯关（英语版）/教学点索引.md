# 教学点索引（源自联网调研的语料库）

> 本文件记录 2026-11-16 联网查证到的权威英语教学资源，以及每类干扰项对应的**真实语料出处**。
> 用于：出题时查证正确项是否真实在用；写解析时标注依据来源。

---

## 一、核心语料库（可长期引用）

| 资源 | 内容 | 用途 |
|---|---|---|
| **Cambridge Learner Corpus** | 5500 万词 / 20 万份考卷 / 215 国 / 143 母语；其中 3200 万词做了错误编码 | 查证学习者真实错误分布 |
| **English Grammar Profile (EGP)** | O'Keeffe & Mark 剑桥，按 CEFR A1–C2 列出学习者真实已掌握的语法点，共 1239 条 | 判断某语法点该在哪个级别教 |
| **English Vocabulary Profile (EVP)** | CEFR 分级词汇表 | 生词本的难度分级 |
| **CEFR 语用能力描述** | B2：能在正式/非正式语域中"confidently, clearly and politely"表达；C1：能识别习语与口语表达 | 语域类干扰项的依据 |
| **languages.dk Scenarios** | 10 个旅游服务场景的完整对话（AIRPORT / HOTEL / RESTAURANT / TRAVEL AGENCY / INFORMATION OFFICE 等） | 服务场景正确项的直接来源 |
| **Cambridge 用语库研究（Pawley & Syder 1983, nativelike selection）** | 母语者不是逐词造句，而是从共享的惯用短语库中挑选现成块 | 教学应教 chunk 而非单词 |

## 二、中文母语者负迁移十大错误（Cambridge / MLJ / SJSU 联合资料）

> 来源：San José State University《Guide for Multilingual Student Writing》、
> Chan (2004) *The Modern Language Journal*（香港 710 名学习者研究）、
> 台湾常见错误汇编。

| # | 错误类型 | 学习者常写 | 自然英语 |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 无过去式 | `Yesterday I go to the office.` | `Yesterday I went to the office.` |
| 2 | 缺第三人称 -s | `My boss like long meetings.` | `My boss likes long meetings.` |
| 3 | 缺复数 -s | `I have three dog.` | `I have three dogs.` |
| 4 | 不可数名词加复数 | `I need more informations.` | `I need more information.` |
| 5 | 缺冠词 | `She is good teacher.` | `She is a good teacher.` |
| 6 | he/she 混用 | `My mom said he is busy.` | `My mom said she is busy.` |
| 7 | There have | `There have many cafés near my office.` | `There are many cafés near my office.` |
| 8 | Although…but | `Although it was raining, but we went out.` | `Although it was raining, we went out.` |
| 9 | Because…so | `Because I was tired, so I went home.` | `Because I was tired, I went home.` |
| 10 | 介词误用 | `He works in TSMC.` | `He works at TSMC.` |

**其他高价值负迁移**（来自 FCE 语料库中国学习者写作分析）：
- 疑问句语序：`You can tell me what is the deadline?` → `Can you tell me what the deadline is?`
- 话题评论结构：`This movie I watched yesterday.` → `I watched this movie yesterday.`
- 缺 be 动词：`This movie very good.` → `This movie is really good.`
- 缺主语：`Is raining outside.` → `It is raining outside.`

## 三、Chinglish 搭配对照表（L2 干扰项取材库）

> 来源：ESLVista 对母语者习惯搭配的整理 + collocation 教学文献。

| 学习者常说 | 自然英语 | 错因 |
|---|---|---|
| `Please open the air conditioner.` | `Could you turn on the air conditioning?` | 「开」分化为 turn on / open / start |
| `The price is very expensive.` | `It's really expensive.` | 价格用 high/low，物品才用 expensive |
| `I learned a lot of knowledge.` | `I learned a lot.` | learn 与 knowledge 极少搭配 |
| `I have a lot of pressure.` | `I'm under a lot of pressure.` | 固定短语 |
| `Please reply me as soon as possible.` | `Could you let me know when you get a chance?` | reply 不接人作宾语 + 语域 |
| `My English is very poor, please forgive me.` | `Sorry, my English isn't perfect.` | 过度正式且自我贬低 |
| `discuss about the proposal` | `discuss the proposal` | discuss 后不加 about |
| `do a decision` | `make a decision` | 固定搭配 |
| `check my manager` | `check with my manager` | 缺介词 |
| `help me to check` | `help me check` | help 后接动词原形 |
| `raise up the problem` | `raise the problem` | 副词冗余 |
| `take over the task` | `take on the task` | 搭配错误 |

## 四、语域敏感表达（设计 L1 干扰项用）

| 场合 | 正式/公文 | 自然/口语 | 中性安全 |
|---|---|---|---|
| 服务请求 | `I would like to procure…` | `Can I grab…` | `Could I have…?` |
| 投诉 | `I demand a formal redress.` | `This is unacceptable.` | `I'm sorry, but…` |
| 拒绝 | `I must decline, as my workload is at capacity.` | `I can't take this on.` | `I'm afraid I can't…` |
| 提问 | `Would you be so kind as to elucidate…?` | `So, what's the deal with…?` | `Could you tell me…?` |
| 汇报 | `I hereby summarise the principal findings.` | `So basically the numbers are…` | `I'll cover three things…` |

## 六、给学习者的解析句式模板

> 母语者的教学反馈习惯是**先肯定，再定位，再给替代**。解析统一采用：

```
正确项用 <chunk>，是 <场景> 里最自然的说法。
<字母> 用 <表达>，<语域/搭配/功能问题>。
<字母> <问题>。
<字母> <问题>。
```

避免使用「这是错误答案」这类无信息量的表述；要指出**错在哪一环**。
